Danyang Li | 李丹阳

The MobiSense Lab, THU

Hi! My name is Danyang Li (李丹阳). I am a final-year Ph.D. student at the School of Software in Tsinghua University, where I am advised by Prof. Zheng Yang. Since joining the MobiSense Lab in 2019, I have completed my M.E. degree in Software Engineering and am continuing my research under Professor Yang's guidance. Before that, I received my B.E. degree from the School of Software in Yanshan University in 2019.


Address: Room 11-211, East Main Building, Tsinghua University

  • Visual SLAM systems, Event Camera
